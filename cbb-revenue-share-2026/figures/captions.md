**fig-01-share-paid** (in "Who got paid in men's basketball", after the top-tenth paragraph)
Share of each conference's men's basketball roster that got any revenue-share payment from July 1, 2025 to April 6, 2026: Big 12 79.0% (196 of 248), ACC 61.2% (169 of 276), Big Ten 59.4% (165 of 278), SEC 45.1% (114 of 253), and 62.8% (2,303 of 3,667) outside the four power conferences. The label on each bar is the median rostered player, counting the players paid nothing: $100,000 in the Big 12, $38,000 in the ACC, $48,000 in the Big Ten, $0 in the SEC and $8,500 outside the four. Percentiles by conference, not totals. Source: College Sports Commission, School Revenue Share Compensation Report, accessed November 17, 2026.

**fig-02-paid-range** (in "Who got paid in men's basketball", after the paragraph on players outside the four)
Men's basketball players who got paid, by conference: the dots mark the lines for the bottom quarter, the median, the top quarter and the top tenth of paid players. The SEC's bottom-quarter line, $117,500, is the highest of the four (Big Ten $80,000, Big 12 $54,000, ACC $50,000); its paid median is $175,000. At the top tenth the Big 12 leads at $662,500, then the ACC at $648,000, the SEC at $599,500 and the Big Ten at $559,500. Payments made July 1, 2025 to April 6, 2026. Source: College Sports Commission, School Revenue Share Compensation Report, accessed November 17, 2026.
