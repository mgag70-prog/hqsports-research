The SEC paid 45.1% of its men's basketball players any revenue share through April 6, 114 of 253, per the College Sports Commission's payment data. The Big 12 paid 79.0%, 196 of 248.

## Post 2

So the median rostered player, counting the ones paid nothing: Big 12 $100,000, Big Ten $48,000, ACC $38,000, SEC $0. Every school worked under the same $20.5 million cap.

## Post 3

Attach: `figures/fig-02-paid-range.png`

The SEC pays the players it does pay. Roughly three in four paid SEC players got at least $117,500, the highest floor of the four power conferences, and its paid median is $175,000.

## Post 4

Football runs on a different clock: payments made or scheduled in calendar 2026, incentives included, against basketball payments made through April 6. Power-conference football paid or scheduled payments to 75.1% of its players.
