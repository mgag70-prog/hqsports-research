**fig-01-peer-table** (in "What I asked for, and what everyone has said", after the second paragraph)
Six Big Ten public schools and what each has put on the record about revenue sharing, as of October 13, 2026: whether its FY2025 NCAA financial report is public on its own site, its stated 2025-26 revenue-sharing total, and whether it has disclosed the split by sport. Ohio State's $18 million is payments; the other $2.5 million of its cap went to scholarships. Michigan's, Michigan State's and Iowa's totals are governing-board budget lines; Michigan's regents projected $18.0 million actually spent. Indiana's and Illinois's totals are their athletic directors' statements. No school in the group has published dollars by sport; Michigan, Indiana and Illinois have each said football gets about 75 percent. Source: data/peers.csv, with a URL, date and exact quote for every cell.

**fig-02-category-44** (in "The form", after the every-cell paragraph)
Category 44 of the NCAA financial report, "Institutional NIL Revenue Share," as Illinois filed it for FY2025, by program: $0 in every column, because payments began July 1, 2025, the first day of FY2026. The form also carries a twelve-sport table on page 74, baseball through wrestling, with men's and women's columns; its sport rows are blank and its subtotals read 0. Ohio State's and Indiana's FY2025 reports carry the same zero row. The FY2024 form had no Category 44. The FY2026 column is the report the October 12 letter says "will be posted on this page when they become available." Source: NCAA MFRS reports, FY2025: Illinois, pages 16, 74 and 94; Ohio State; Indiana. FOIA 26-2759 response.

**fig-03-revenue-expenses** (in "Illinois's budget, for scale", after the first paragraph)
Illinois athletics' operating revenue and expenses, FY2024 and FY2025, from the NCAA financial reports the department posts. Revenue rose from $174.1 million to $194.8 million, 11.9%; expenses rose from $169.5 million to $189.9 million, 12.0%. The surplus went from $4.6 million to $4.9 million. For scale, Ohio State's FY2025 revenue was $336.1 million and Indiana's $183.4 million. Fiscal years end June 30; FY2025 ended the day before revenue-sharing payments began. Source: NCAA MFRS reports, Illinois FY2024 (posted January 23, 2025) and FY2025 (posted January 29, 2026); Ohio State and Indiana FY2025.

**fig-04-surplus-by-program** (in "Illinois's budget, for scale", after the where-the-money-came-from paragraph)
FY2025 operating surplus or deficit by program, as the NCAA report divides it. Football cleared $38.7 million and men's basketball $16.7 million, $55.3 million together. Women's basketball lost $4.9 million, the other sports $25.4 million, and expenses not tied to any program ran $20.2 million, so the department's net surplus was $4.9 million. Source: NCAA MFRS report, Illinois FY2025, page 94.
