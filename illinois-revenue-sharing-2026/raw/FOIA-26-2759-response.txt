    I write to respond to your request dated and received in my office October 5, 2026, under
the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) in which you requested:

        “… the following records from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign Division of
        Intercollegiate Athletics:

        1. Records showing the total amount of direct payments to student-athletes under the
        House v. NCAA settlement (revenue sharing) for fiscal year 2026 (July 1, 2025 – June 30,
        2026), broken down by sport.
        2. Records showing the budgeted or planned allocation of revenue-sharing payments by
        sport for fiscal year 2027 (2026-27).
        3. The fiscal year 2025 and fiscal year 2026 NCAA Membership Financial Reporting
        System (MFRS) reports submitted by the University.

        The records are for reporting in Ledger & Whistle (ledgerandwhistle.substack.com). I
        am not requesting individual athlete names or individual contract amounts; aggregate
        figures by sport are sufficient.”

   Points 1 and 2 of your request are denied pursuant to the following section(s) of the Act:

        •    140/7(1)(g) that exempts from disclosure “Trade secrets and commercial or financial
             information obtained from a person or business where the trade secrets or
             commercial or financial information are furnished under a claim that they are
             proprietary, privileged or confidential, and that, or where, disclosure of the trade
             secrets or commercial or financial information would cause competitive harm to the
             person or business…” Given the institution-specific discretion in allocating revenue-
             sharing payments across sports and between student-athletes, the requested
             information is inextricably linked to the competitive strategies of the University. The
             release of these records would cause competitive harm by allowing other institutions
             to learn, replicate and/or exploit University of Illinois allocation strategies and
             priorities.
