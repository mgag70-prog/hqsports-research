# The Ledger: October 2026

*Nine pieces in October, ten calls on the record, one already at risk. Here's the scorecard, and the misses will go on it too.*

Every piece I publish ends with a call: something specific, and the event that settles it. A call is only worth making if somebody keeps score, so I'm keeping it in public, and the misses stay on the page next to the hits. Starting today, The Ledger is a Note every Monday, with a recap article like this one each month.

## What I published in October

- [Penn State's $49.7 million buyout cost $9 million. The contract said it would.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/penn-states-497-million-buyout-cost) The offset clause did nearly all of the work.
- [I paid for the same four Wrigley seats for 18 years.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/i-paid-for-the-same-four-wrigley) After inflation, the price rose 13.0% in the first 11 years and 44.9% in the last six.
- [Baseball's owners want a salary cap. The last league to get one grew slower than baseball for seven years.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/baseballs-owners-want-a-salary-cap) The NHL's 2005 cap is the only test inside Forbes' valuation record, and it ran the other way.
- [Four MLB teams' season-ticket contracts mention a lockout. Only one promises your money back.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/four-mlb-teams-season-ticket-contracts) Of 30 clubs' public terms, 19 leave canceled games to the club, say sales are final, or have none I could find.
- [Eight fewer games is 3.5 million fewer tickets.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/eight-fewer-games-is-35-million-fewer) The plan trades shared local gate for national television inventory, and every national deal on the record expires after 2028.
- [No Big Ten school has published revenue sharing by sport in dollars.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/no-big-ten-school-has-published-revenue) Illinois denied my records request on October 12, but it posts the NCAA form with a line built for that number.
- [Baseball's best team would have been under the owners' salary floor.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/baseballs-best-team-would-have-been) The Brewers went 103-59 on a payroll $16.5 million under the proposed $171.2 million floor.
- [The Dodgers are already $133 million over a 2027 salary cap, before they sign anyone.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/the-dodgers-are-already-133-million) On signed contracts, five clubs are over the proposed $245.3 million cap; add Spotrac's projected arbitration salaries and it's eight.
- [Taxpayers have committed $13.5 billion to 19 stadiums and arenas that haven't opened yet.](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/taxpayers-have-committed-135-billion) Across 38 deals the public put in $18.3 billion, and only three of those deals went to a public vote.

## What moved in October

The Brewers won the World Series 4-1, closing it out in Game 5 on October 28 with a 4-1 win. They did it on a 2026 payroll of $154.71 million, under the owners' proposed floor ([floor piece](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/baseballs-best-team-would-have-been)). Before Game 1, Rob Manfred said of small-market clubs, "Once every 40 years is not enough" ([cap piece](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/the-dodgers-are-already-133-million)). Then one won it. That grades none of the calls, since the floor call is about the first season under a new agreement, but it's an awkward month for the case that a cheap roster can't win.

On October 29, Greg Hinz of Crain's Chicago Business reported that the Bears were nearing a deal to stay in Illinois, most likely in Arlington Heights, with a stadium exempt from property taxes ([stadium piece](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/taxpayers-have-committed-135-billion)). Nothing is announced, and lawmakers could vote as soon as November. The Bears aren't among the 19 projects my stadium call covers.

The labor agreement expires at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 1 ([season-ticket piece](https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/four-mlb-teams-season-ticket-contracts)). Six of the ten calls depend on what replaces it.

Tell me in the comments: which of these would you bet against?
