## Post 1

Attach: `figures/fig-01-2026-vs-2028-lines.png`

MLB pushed its salary cap back to 2028 on Tuesday, at $258.2 million with a $180.2 million floor. On 2026 payrolls that's 8 clubs over and 12 under, all 20 of them on the old list too. Only Atlanta got off it, by $0.68 million.

## Post 2

The lines moved with the date. The cap rose $12.9 million and the floor $9.0 million, each 5.3%, so the floor is still 69.8% of the cap. That's the same system on a later date.

## Post 3

The new part: a club could spend as little as 90% of the floor in a year, $162.18 million, if it meets a multi-year minimum. It rescues nobody on this year's list, because all 12 were below that too. It does cut their bill from $656.3 million to $440.0 million.

## Post 4

Attach: `figures/fig-02-2027-vs-2028-lines.png`

On Spotrac's 2027 projections: 7 clubs over the 2028 cap, 12 under the floor. The names shift, though. Houston joins the top group, and the champion Brewers project at $192.41 million, $12.21 million above the floor.

## Post 5

Attach: `figures/fig-03-calendar.png`

A free agent's window to sign with a new club runs November 2 to December 1: 30 days, counting both ends. A lockout would freeze transactions and bar players from team facilities, and most people expect the Winter Meetings to be cancelled.

## Post 6
