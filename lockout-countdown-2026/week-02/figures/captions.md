**fig-01-2021-vs-2026-dollars** (in "The money that has moved this time", after the cover line)
Free-agent dollars committed before the lockout: more than $1.7 billion in November 2021, by CBS Sports' count, against $11 million in reported terms in 2026 through November 10, 0.64% of it. The 2026 figure counts only signings with reported terms, and Emilio Pagán's one-year deal with the Tigers is the only one through November 10. Source: CBS Sports, December 2, 2021; MLB Trade Rumors, November 10, 2026.

**fig-02-top50-predictions** (in "What the market could still do", after the first paragraph)
MLB Trade Rumors' 50 contract predictions, published November 2, 2026, as a running total by rank. Red is Tarik Skubal's predicted $425 million, 17.8% of the $2.38 billion total. The dashed line is November 2021's $1.7 billion, which the running total passes at No. 19. These are predictions for the whole offseason, not for the 20 days before the agreement expires. Source: MLB Trade Rumors, November 2, 2026; CBS Sports, December 2, 2021.

**fig-03-calendar** (in "The next dates", after the paragraph)
The calendar from November 11 to December 9. With 20 days left, the agreement expires at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 1, and a lockout is expected December 2. The qualifying-offer and Rule 5 protection deadlines fall on November 17, the non-tender deadline on November 20, and the Winter Meetings are set for December 7-9 in San Diego. Source: Just Baseball, October 13, 2026; AP, November 2, 2026; ESPN, November 3, 2026.
