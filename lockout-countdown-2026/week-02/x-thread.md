## Post 1

Before the 2021 lockout, clubs committed more than $1.7 billion to free agents in November. This year the first significant deal took nine days: Emilio Pagán to the Tigers, one year at about $11 million.

## Post 2

That's the whole reported total through November 10, $11 million, or 0.64% of November 2021. The two other signings I've found came without reported terms, so they count as zero.

## Post 3

Attach: `figures/fig-02-top50-predictions.png`

The predictions run much bigger. MLB Trade Rumors' top 50 adds up to $2.38 billion, 1.39 times November 2021, and Tarik Skubal is 17.8% of it on his own. That's for the whole offseason, though, not the 20 days before the agreement expires.

## Post 4

Skubal is hoping to sign before the lockout, and the Mets have a meeting with him set for next week. Others may wait. An official told CBS Sports: "We're really operating under uncertainty now."
