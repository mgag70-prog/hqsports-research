**fig-01-payrolls** (in "Checking Sword's claim", after the cover line)
All 30 clubs' 2026 luxury-tax payrolls, highest to lowest. The Dodgers' $433.06 million is 5.13 times the Marlins' $84.36 million, a gap of $348.7 million; the median club spent $210.4 million. **fig-02-leagues** (in "Against the other leagues", after the league figures)
How wide each league's rules let payrolls spread, against what baseball's actually did in 2026. NFL: the 2026 cap of $301.2 million over a cash floor of 90% of the caps, measured across 2024-2026, 1.11 times. NBA: the $164.961 million cap over the $148.465 million minimum team salary, 1.11 times, or 1.49 times with the $221.686 million second apron as the ceiling. NHL: the $104.0 million upper limit over the $76.9 million lower limit, 1.35 times. MLB: the Dodgers over the Marlins, 5.13 times. Source: NFL-NFLPA CBA; Pro Football Rumors, February 27, 2026; NBA, June 30, 2026; NHL, May 6, 2026; Spotrac.
