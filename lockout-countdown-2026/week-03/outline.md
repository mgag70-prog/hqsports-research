## 4. The nuclear option (short)
- WSJ (Jared Diamond) via MLBTR Nov 12: MLB is preparing for the possibility the union files a disclaimer of interest during a lockout, stops acting as the bargaining unit, and players sue under antitrust law. The MLBPA has never done it; the Curt Flood Act of 1998 is why it's possible now; likely only deep into a lockout.
- Precedent, as MLBTR reports it: NFLPA early 1990s (jury award $543K tripled to $1.63MM); NFLPA 2011 (injunction lifting the lockout, reversed by the Eighth Circuit).
