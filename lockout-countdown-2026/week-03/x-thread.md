## Post 1

MLB's new video makes the case for a salary cap to fans. Morgan Sword's line: "MLB has the widest payroll gap in sports, and it's not even close." On 2026 payrolls, the Dodgers spent $433.06 million and the Marlins $84.36 million: 5.13 times.

## Post 2

Attach: `figures/fig-02-leagues.png`

The other leagues' rules don't come close. The NHL's upper limit is 1.35 times its lower limit. The NBA's second apron is 1.49 times its minimum team salary. The NFL's cap and cash floor work out to 1.11 times.

## Post 3

So Sword's line holds. The video skips its own champion, though: the Brewers won the World Series with the No. 19 payroll, $154.71 million, from the bottom half of that 5.13-to-1 spread.

## Post 4

Meanwhile, the money. By my count clubs have committed $158.75 million in reported free-agent terms through November 17, 9.29% of November 2021. David Bednar and Ryan Jeffers took the $23.15 million qualifying offer; the other six declined.

## Post 5

The agreement expires at 11:59 p.m. ET on December 1, and MLB is already planning for a union disclaimer of interest, what one academic called "the nuclear option."
