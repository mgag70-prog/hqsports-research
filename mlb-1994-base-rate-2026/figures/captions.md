**fig-02-recovery** (replaces the base-rate table in "The base rate")
Per-game attendance after each of the three stoppages that cost regular-season games, indexed to the last full season before it at 100 and plotted against seasons after that baseline. The 1972 strike, 86 games lost, took attendance down 3.7% from 1971 and the 1981 strike, 712 games lost, took it down 6.8% from 1980; both lines were back above 100 two seasons after their baselines, in 1973 and 1982, and are drawn only through the fourth season. The 1994-95 strike, 921 games lost by the AP's count, is indexed to 1993, the last full season: 1995 came in 19.2% below it, and the line didn't get back above 100 until 2006, thirteen seasons later. The 1994 point sits above 100 because the league was on a record pace when the strike began. Measured against that 1994 pace instead of 1993, recovery takes twelve seasons, also to 2006. Zoomed axis; it does not start at zero. Source: Baseball Reference, Major League year-by-year totals, retrieved September 24, 2026. Games lost for 1994-95 are the AP's count, 669 in 1994 and 252 in 1995. Indexes calculated.

**fig-05-long-view** (new, in "Where baseball stands going in")
MLB per-game attendance by season, 1970 through 2026. The three strikes that cost games are shaded; the dashed line marks the 2021-22 lockout, which cost none. Attendance peaked in 2007 at 32,696 and no season since has matched it. 2026, a season in progress, is at 29,460, which is 5.7% below the 1994 pace of 31,256 and 9.9% below the peak. There's no 2020 point because no fans were admitted, and 2021's 18,651 stands alone because capacity limits held down the early part of that season. Dotted leaders mark the three expansion seasons in the series, 1977, 1993, and 1998. The 1993 expansion from 26 to 28 teams came with a 16.7% jump in per-game attendance, from 26,529 to 30,964, and that season is the baseline for the 1994-95 recovery. Zoomed axis; it does not start at zero. Source: Baseball Reference, Major League year-by-year totals, retrieved September 24, 2026. Gaps against the 1994 pace and the 2007 peak calculated.
