**fig-01-dodgers-stack** (in "The Dodgers", after the first paragraph)
The Dodgers' 2027 luxury-tax payroll built up step by step against the owners' proposed cap of $245.3 million. Guaranteed contracts alone come to $355.84 million, $110.5 million over; Kyle Tucker ($57.195 million) and Shohei Ohtani ($46.08 million) are $103.28 million of that, 42.1% of the cap. Adding benefits, minor league salaries and the pre-arbitration bonus pool gives $378.76 million on signed contracts, $133.5 million over; Spotrac's projected arbitration and pre-arbitration salaries take it to $393.81 million, $148.5 million over. Source: Spotrac 2027 tax tracker and Dodgers tax page, read October 27, 2026; cap: AP, October 24, 2026, and CBS Sports, May 28, 2026. Projected figures are Spotrac's estimates.

**fig-02-all-30** (in "Everyone over the line", after the first paragraph)
All 30 clubs' 2027 luxury-tax payrolls, sorted by projected payroll, with the proposed cap of $245.3 million and floor of $171.2 million. Solid bars are signed contracts; outlined extensions add Spotrac's projected arbitration and pre-arbitration salaries. Five clubs are over the cap on signed contracts, $249.6 million in all; eight are over on projection, $539.1 million in all. Twelve project under the floor. Source: Spotrac, read October 27, 2026; AP, October 24, 2026; CBS Sports, May 28, 2026. Rosters are incomplete in October.

**fig-03-over-max** (in "Contracts the proposal wouldn't allow", after the second paragraph)
The eight players whose 2027 luxury-tax figures exceed 15% of the proposed cap ($36.795 million), the starting maximum for a free agent changing teams under the owners' June proposal. Seven also exceed 16% ($39.248 million), the starting maximum for a re-signed "Cornerstone Player"; Jacob deGrom, at $37.0 million, is between the two lines. Kyle Tucker leads at $57.195 million. Source: Spotrac 2027 player rankings by luxury tax, read October 27, 2026; limits: CBS Sports bargaining timeline, June 25 entry, and Sportsnaut, June 26, 2026. The limits as described apply to new free-agent contracts; how they would treat these existing deals hasn't been said.

**fig-04-over-265** (in "Contracts the proposal wouldn't allow", after the third paragraph)
The eighteen contracts still running in 2027 whose total value exceeds $265 million, the largest contract the owners' proposal would allow. Juan Soto's $765 million is 2.9 times the maximum; the Dodgers, Yankees and Padres hold three each, and Kansas City holds Bobby Witt Jr.'s $288.8 million. Source: Spotrac contracts by total value, read October 27, 2026; maximum: AP, October 24, 2026. Values are nominal contract totals.
