## Post 2

Attach: `figures/fig-01-dodgers-stack.png`

The Dodgers: $355.84 million in guaranteed contracts alone, $110.5 million over the cap. Signed contracts plus benefits: $378.76 million, $133.5 million over. Projected: $393.81 million. Tucker and Ohtani together count $103.28 million, 42.1% of the cap.

The proposal starts a free agent changing teams at 15% of the cap, $36.795 million. Eight players already count more in 2027, led by Tucker at $57.195 million and Soto at $51.0 million. Seven clear the 16% line for re-signing a "Cornerstone Player" too.

Attach: `figures/fig-04-over-265.png`

Eighteen contracts running in 2027 are bigger than $265 million, the largest the proposal allows. Soto's $765 million is 2.9 times it. Nobody has said what happens to them. In 2005 the NHL cut every existing contract 24%. That's the precedent, not a prediction.
