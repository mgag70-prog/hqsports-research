**fig-01-final-four** (in "The final four, against the lines", after the first paragraph)
The four League Championship Series clubs' 2026 luxury-tax payrolls against the owners' proposed 2027 cap of $245.3 million and floor of $171.2 million. The Dodgers, $433.06 million, were $187.8 million over the cap; the Brewers, $154.71 million, were $16.5 million under the floor; the Rays, $113.23 million, $58.0 million under; the Guardians, $92.58 million, $78.6 million under. The Dodgers' payroll is $72.5 million more than the other three combined. Source: Spotrac team tax tracker, end of the 2026 regular season, read October 19, 2026, including player benefits; CBS Sports, May 28, 2026, for the lines. The lines are 2027 figures laid over 2026 payrolls.

**fig-02-all-30** (in "All 30 clubs", after the first paragraph)
All 30 clubs' 2026 luxury-tax payrolls, sorted, with the proposed cap and floor. Nine clubs were over the cap, twelve under the floor and nine between. The four League Championship Series clubs are in red; the Brewers rank 19th and the Guardians 29th. Bringing every club inside the lines would cut $627.3 million from the nine over the cap and add $548.3 million at the twelve under the floor, $79.0 million less for players in all. Source: Spotrac, read October 19, 2026; CBS Sports, May 28, 2026. Calculated.

**fig-03-wins-vs-payroll** (in "Spending still buys wins, on average", after the first paragraph)
Regular-season wins against 2026 luxury-tax payroll for all 30 clubs, the cap and floor drawn as vertical lines, playoff clubs filled. The nine over-cap clubs averaged 88.3 wins and the twelve under-floor clubs 80.0, but the best record, Milwaukee's 103-59, and the third-best, Tampa Bay's 98-64, both sit left of the floor, with the Dodgers' 100-62 far right of the cap. The Mets, $359.48 million and 74-88, are the most expensive club that missed October. Source: Spotrac, read October 19, 2026; CBS Sports bracket, October 18, 2026.

**fig-04-change-to-comply** (in "What a floor does", after the first paragraph)
What each club would have had to cut or add in 2026 to sit inside the proposed 2027 lines. Cuts, in red, total $627.3 million across nine clubs, $187.8 million of it the Dodgers'. Additions, in navy, total $548.3 million across twelve clubs; the four largest are the Marlins at $86.8 million, the Guardians at $78.6 million, the Cardinals at $62.4 million and the Rays at $58.0 million. The Brewers' addition is $16.5 million. Source: Spotrac, read October 19, 2026; CBS Sports, May 28, 2026. Calculated; an illustration, not a forecast.
