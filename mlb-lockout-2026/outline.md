# Outline: what your season-ticket contract says about a lockout

Status: research done October 8, 2026 (evening). Data: data/club-terms.csv (30 clubs, verbatim quotes checked by model/build_terms_table.py). Sources: data/source-urls.csv; raw pages in raw/ (gitignore raw/).

## Working headline options
1. Four MLB teams' season-ticket contracts mention a lockout. Only one promises your money back.
2. Read your season-ticket contract before December 1
3. If baseball locks out, here's what 30 teams say happens to your season-ticket money

## The finding (numbers from data/club-terms.csv)
- Public season-ticket or ticket terms for all 30 clubs, read October 8, 2026.
- 4 of 30 name a lockout or strike: Cubs, Reds, Diamondbacks, Yankees.
  - Reds: fees cut pro rata per canceled home game; credit, or a refund within 60 days of MLB confirming the cancellation, on written request. The only club that names a lockout and promises cash back on a clock.
  - Diamondbacks: credit toward future games. No refund.
  - Cubs: invoice fees due on time "regardless of" a lockout. If fewer than 41 home games are played, the club keeps the money and applies it to the next season and extends the license. If 41 or more are played, it's a "full Season," and paragraph 14's first sentence says the club owes no refund for games canceled by a force majeure event, which it defines to include lockouts. Read as written: up to 40 canceled home games with no refund or credit.
  - Yankees: club not liable for force majeure delays, tickets "not subject to any refund," and the club is not "obligated to settle or vote to settle any strike, lockout or work stoppage."
- 7 promise a credit or refund for games canceled and not rescheduled, without naming a lockout: Brewers, Royals (credit or refund incl. parking), Giants (credit; refund if you ask within 30 days), Angels (refund if you ask within 30 days or it is "forever waived"), Phillies (credit; cash only if you quit renewing), Braves and Blue Jays (general ticket terms: automatic refund, no credits).
- 10 leave it to the club or MLB: Cardinals, Tigers, Marlins, Dodgers, Twins, White Sox, Rockies, plus Mariners, Padres and Orioles (membership fees not refundable; ticket remedy not on the page).
- 7 say sales are final or have no canceled-game clause on the public page: Astros, Red Sox, Pirates, Guardians, Nationals, Rays, Rangers.
- 2 have no public season-ticket terms found: Mets, Athletics.
- Timing: where a deadline exists, it is more often on the fan than on the club. Angels and Giants: fan must ask within 30 days. Reds: club pays within 60 days. Blue Jays: "as soon as" 60 days. Nobody else states a payment clock.

## Structure
1. Lede: the CBA expires 11:59 p.m. ET December 1. Renewal invoices are already out. What does the contract say happens to the money?
2. The four that name a lockout, best to worst for the fan (Reds, Diamondbacks, Cubs, Yankees). Quote each.
3. The Cubs' 41-game line. 81 home games now; up to 40 can be canceled with no refund. Under MLB's October 8 proposal (154 games from 2029, 77 home dates), the same line would allow 36. Tie to the 154-game news.
4. The seven that promise credit or refund for canceled games without naming a lockout. Note: these clauses aren't written for labor stoppages, and force majeure language elsewhere could be read against them. We read them as written.
5. The other 19: discretion, sales final, or nothing public.
6. What happened last time: 2021-22 lockout (Dec 2, 2021 to Mar 10, 2022). Brewers gave credit or refund incl. parking; Reds credited automatically; the 162-game season was preserved with makeups, so no club's season-ticket clause was tested on a canceled game. (Sources in project memo.)
7. Disclosure: Matt held Cubs season tickets 1998-2021 and gave them up over 2020 refund handling (link the Wrigley piece).
8. What to do: read your terms; note any request deadline; ask your rep in writing which clause applies to a lockout.
9. Prediction on the record (Matt to confirm wording): if regular-season home games are canceled in 2027, no club that keeps money under its terms changes them mid-stoppage; refunds, where they happen, run past 60 days for most fans.
