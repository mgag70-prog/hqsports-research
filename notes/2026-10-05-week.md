## Monday, October 5

Attach: `mlb-cap-valuations-2026/figures/fig-02-ratio.png`

The average baseball team was worth 1.81 times the average NHL team on Forbes' 2004 lists, the last before hockey's salary cap. Seven years into the cap, on the 2011 lists, it was 2.18 times. The gap widened under the cap. It closed later, to 1.18 by 2025, in the years hockey's national TV deals arrived.
