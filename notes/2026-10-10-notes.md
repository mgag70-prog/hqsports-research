## Saturday, October 10: the deadline is on you

Attach: `mlb-lockout-2026/figures/fig-03-refund-clocks.png`

I read all 30 MLB clubs' public season-ticket terms this week. Only 4 state any refund clock at all, and two of those run against the fan. At the Angels, refunds "must be requested within thirty (30) days of the announced cancellation of the Event or the refund is forever waived." The Giants give you 30 days to ask too. The Reds are the one club that puts the clock on itself: 60 days to pay. The other 26 say nothing about when.

## Sunday, October 11: eight fewer games, same price?

Attach: `mlb-lockout-2026/figures/fig-04-cubs-line.png`

MLB's October 8 proposal cuts the season to 154 games from 2029, contingent on a salary cap. That's 77 home dates instead of 81, so a season-ticket plan loses 4 home games. Nobody has said whether plan prices fall with them. The Cubs' 41-game "full season" line doesn't move: 40 cancellable games today, 36 on the new schedule. If your plan lost 4 games, would you expect the price to drop?
