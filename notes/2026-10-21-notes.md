## Saturday, October 24: the honest counterpoint

Attach: `mlb-floor-2026/figures/fig-03-wins-vs-payroll.png`

The honest counterpoint to my own piece. The nine clubs over the proposed cap averaged 88.3 wins this year; the twelve under the floor averaged 80.0. Money still buys wins on average, and a chart with a cap line on it shouldn't pretend otherwise. The Mets are the other half of the lesson: the second-highest payroll in baseball, $359.48M, a 74-88 record, and no October. Two pennant winners under the floor is a story. It isn't a trend.
