## Wednesday, November 11: lockout countdown week 2 is out

Attach: `lockout-countdown-2026/week-02/figures/cover-fal-1080x1080.png`

New this morning: 20 days until baseball's labor deal expires. Before the 2021 lockout, clubs committed more than $1.7 billion to free agents in November. This year the first significant deal took nine days: Emilio Pagán to the Tigers, one year at about $11 million. MLB Trade Rumors' top 50 predictions add up to $2.38 billion, and Tarik Skubal is 17.8% of it. The money hasn't moved yet. I don't think it gets to $1.7 billion before December 2.
