Pete Crow-Armstrong's 2026 pay, about $9.45 million: his $894,000 salary, the $5 million signing bonus from his extension, and North Side Baseball's estimate of his pre-arbitration pool payout, $3.56 million, split into $2.5 million for the MVP and about $1.06 million for WAR. **fig-02-pool** (in "The pool is on the table", after the second paragraph)
The pre-arbitration bonus pool: $50 million under the current agreement, $65 million in MLB's November 3 offer, 30% more, and $180 million in the union's counter, 3.6 times the current pool. The shaded part of the $50 million bar is the $12.75 million clubs paid in award bonuses in 2025, 25.5% of the pool. Source: ESPN, November 3, 2026; AP via ESPN, July 24, 2026.
