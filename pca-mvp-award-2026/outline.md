## 2. What the award pays, and from where
- 2026 salary: $894,000 ($114K above the $780,000 minimum). His extension starts in 2027, so the season was paid at pre-arb rates (link the late-Sept piece: the contract's MVP escalators start with 2027 voting).
- The MVP triggers $2.5 million from the pre-arbitration bonus pool: $50 million a year, every club pays in roughly $1.67 million, and it goes to pre-arb players even if they've signed early extensions (MLBTR/AP).
- North Side Baseball's estimate: about $3.56 million total ($2.5M award + a bit over $1M WAR share, his 10.2 jWAR = 3.2% of the top 100). That's 4.0 times his salary, about 7.1% of the whole pool, and more than Paul Skenes's record $3,436,343 in 2025. Label it an estimate; the final list comes later in November.
- Who pays: the pool is funded by all 30 clubs, so the Cubs' share of his payout is about 1/30, roughly $119,000. (Label as arithmetic on "roughly $1.67M each".)
- His 2026 pay adds to about $9.45 million with the $5 million signing bonus (plus a small playoff share not counted). In 2027 his salary is $10 million, 11.2 times this year's.

## 3. The pool is on the table
- The current agreement created the pool ($50M). MLB's November 3 offer: $65 million (30% more). The union's counter: $180 million (3.6 times), per ESPN; AP reported the union's ask as $180 million next year with $15 million annual increases.
- Award money already takes a growing slice: $12.75 million in 2025, a quarter of the pool (25.5%). This year NSB says a record number of award bonuses.
- Neither side's proposal, as reported, says what an MVP would get under a bigger pool. Don't speculate on amounts.
- Link week 2 lockout piece (https://ledgerandwhistle.substack.com/p/20-days-left-and-the-mlb-free-agent) once, for where talks stand.

## 4. What the MVP starts on the contract
- Escalators begin with 2027 voting: each MVP 2027-2030 adds $2M to his 2031 base; each MVP 2027-2031 adds $2M to his 2032 base; smaller bumps for 2nd-10th. Most it can add: $8M in 2031 and $10M in 2032 ($13.0M present value at 6%, from the late-Sept piece). This year's MVP counts for none of it.
- One line on the model: the Sept 14 model had 2027 as a $9.0 million loss for the Cubs versus no extension; the late-Sept piece covered what the season does to the savings. Don't redo it.
