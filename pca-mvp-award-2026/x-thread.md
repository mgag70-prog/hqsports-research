## Post 1

Attach: `figures/fig-01-pay-stack.png`

Pete Crow-Armstrong won the NL MVP with all 30 first-place votes, and he played the season for $894,000. His extension doesn't start until 2027, so the MVP year ran at the pre-arbitration rate.

## Post 2

The award pays him anyway, from baseball's $50 million pre-arbitration bonus pool: $2.5 million for the MVP. North Side Baseball estimates his total from the pool at around $3.56 million, 4.0 times his salary.

## Post 3

All 30 clubs pay into that pool in roughly equal shares, so the Cubs' part of his payout is roughly $119,000. The other 29 clubs cover the rest.

## Post 4

Attach: `figures/fig-02-pool.png`

And the pool is on the bargaining table. MLB offered $65 million, 30% more than now. The union wants $180 million, 3.6 times the current pool. Neither proposal, as reported, says what an MVP would collect from a bigger one.

## Post 5

None of this year's award counts toward the MVP escalators in his contract; those start with 2027 voting.
