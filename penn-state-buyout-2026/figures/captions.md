**fig-01-buyout-schedule** (in "The number everyone quoted", after the Kraft paragraph)
What Penn State owed James Franklin when it fired him on October 12, 2025, by contract year. His contract guaranteed $8.0 million a year through 2031, so the six full years account for $48.0 million and the 80 days left in 2025 for another $1.75 million. The bars sum to $49.75 million, calculated; the figure reported at the time was $49.7 million. Source: contract terms from Onward State, October 8, 2025, and Front Office Sports, October 13, 2025; reported buyout from Front Office Sports, November 17, 2025. The 2025 bar is calculated by day count.

**fig-02-two-readings** (in "What the contract said", after the table comparing the two readings)
Top, on one scale: the buyout as reported, $49.7 million, and the $9.0 million settlement, a gap of $40.7 million (calculated). Bottom, zoomed to the first $20 million: what the offset clause alone would have left Penn State owing under each reading of it. Solid bars are owed whatever happens in 2031, and hatched bars are 2031 itself, owed only if Franklin earns nothing that year. Read as one offset against the whole term, the clause leaves about $0 through 2030 and at most $8.0 million. Read year by year, it leaves $10.0 million through 2030 and at most $18.0 million. The red line marks the settlement. Both readings are calculated from the published salary schedules, and nothing published says which one the two sides used. Source: Front Office Sports for the clause, October 13, 2025, and the reported buyout, November 17, 2025; WSLS for Virginia Tech's terms, November 21, 2025; CBS Sports for the settlement, November 17, 2025.

**fig-03-against-roster** (in "What the coaching change cost, and against what", after the roster paragraph)
Four amounts on one scale. The buyout as reported is $49.7 million. Penn State's 2026 roster budget is drawn as a range because it's an estimate: $29 million to $32 million, from The Athletic's September 16, 2026 report. The cash out for the coaching change is $11.0 million, the $9.0 million settlement plus $2.0 million to Iowa State. Matt Campbell's 2026 pay is $9.0 million, $8.0 million guaranteed plus a $1.0 million retention bonus, assuming the bonus pays for 2026. The reported buyout equals 1.55 to 1.71 years of the roster estimate, and the settlement is 28% to 31% of one year. Source: Front Office Sports, CBS Sports, WPSU and StateCollege.com for the contract figures; The Athletic for the roster estimate. Totals and ratios calculated.
