## 1. The number everyone quoted

- Franklin's 10-year extension, effective January 2022, paid $8.0M a year: $500,000
  base, $6.5M supplemental, $1M life insurance loan (Onward State, October 8, 2025).
- Buyout formula: the remaining base, supplemental and insurance loan through 2031. It
  fell by $8M each January 1.
- Fired October 12, 2025, after a 3-3 start and a loss to Northwestern (Fox Sports).
  Reported buyout $49.7M (Front Office Sports; Athletic Business). Athletic director Pat
  Kraft: athletics, not the university, covers the cost (Athletic Business, October 14,
  2025).
- Context worth one sentence: the reporting at the time framed this against Penn State's
  budget pressure and stadium renovation. **Only include specific budget or debt figures
  if Claude Code verifies them against a primary source; the $500M+ debt figure I found
  is from a secondary report.**

## 2. What Penn State actually paid

- Contract carried offset language: Franklin had to make a good faith effort to find
  another job, and Penn State owed only the difference if the new job paid less (SI,
  October 13, 2025).
- Virginia Tech hired him November 17, 2025, 36 days after the firing. Penn State and
  Franklin settled for $9.0M, which closes the relationship through 2031 (CBS Sports,
  November 17; SI, November 18).
- Savings against $49.7M: $40.7M (Front Office Sports).
- Franklin on leaving the money: quote from CBS Sports ("This place wanted us...").
  Optional, short.

## 3. The two paychecks (the finding)

Virginia Tech contract, five years, $41.75M total, $41M guaranteed (WSLS, November 21,
2025). Annual compensation from the same source:

| Year | Virginia Tech | Penn State owes under the offset | Franklin's total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | $6.0M | $2.0M | $8.0M |
| 2027 | $5.0M | $3.0M | $8.0M |
| 2028 | $4.0M | $4.0M | $8.0M |
| 2029 | $12.75M | $0 | $12.75M |
| 2030 | $13.25M | $0 | $13.25M |

- Offset total for 2026 through 2030: $9.0M, the settlement figure.
- For three years, Virginia Tech's pay plus Penn State's offset equals exactly his Penn
  State salary.
- The Virginia Tech contract is back-loaded. The low years fall exactly where Penn State's
  offset applies; the high years start when the offset would no longer bite.

**Judgment, labeled:** the match suggests the settlement was priced off the offset clause
against a contract structured low in the years Penn State was on the hook. It doesn't
prove anyone designed it that way. Say both.

**What the settlement covered that the offset table doesn't:** the rest of 2025 (he had no
job from October 12 to November 17) and 2031 (Virginia Tech's contract ends in 2030; if
he had no job paying $8M in 2031, Penn State would have owed up to $8M). The settlement
closed both for the same $9.0M. So Penn State paid no more than the offset would have
produced through 2030, and bought certainty on 2031.

**Discrepancy to state:** WSLS's year-by-year figures sum to $41.0M; the reported total is
$41.75M. The difference is likely non-salary compensation or rounding in reporting. Don't
resolve it by assumption; say it's unexplained and that it doesn't touch the offset
arithmetic, which uses the yearly figures.

## 4. Penn State kept the clause

- Matt Campbell, hired December 2025: eight years through 2033, $78.5M total ($70.5M
  base guaranteed plus $1M a year retention). Base $8M in 2026 rising to $9.25M (Onward
  State, December 8; WPSU, December 8, 2025).
- If Penn State fires him: 100% of remaining guaranteed compensation, "subject to
  mitigation" (Onward State).
- Penn State paid Iowa State $2.0M to buy him out.
- The point to land: the clause that turned $49.7M into $9.0M is in the new contract too.

## 5. What the coaching change cost, and against what

- Cash out for the change: $9.0M Franklin settlement + $2.0M Iowa State buyout = $11.0M.
  **Calculated.**
- Campbell's 2026 compensation: $8M base plus $1M retention = $9M. **Calculated; verify
  that retention pays in 2026.**
- Penn State's 2026 roster budget, The Athletic estimate: $29M to $32M (September 16,
  2026). This is an estimate, not a filing; say so, as the roster-budgets piece did.
- Comparison to make plainly: the reported buyout ($49.7M) was larger than a year and a
  half of the roster. The real settlement ($9.0M) is less than a third of one year's.
  **Calculated; Claude Code to generate both ratios from the CSV.**

## 6. What would make this wrong

- Settlement payment terms are unpublished: lump sum or installments changes present
  value but not the total.
- Reported buyout figures vary from $48M to $56.66M depending on timing and source.
- Contract terms come from reporting on documents, not the documents. If Penn State or
  Virginia Tech releases them under a records request, check the schedule.
- The offset arithmetic assumes the clause counted only Virginia Tech base and
  supplemental pay. If it counted bonuses or other compensation, the offset would be
  smaller.

## 7. What this predicts

The next big-name firing with an offset clause will be reported at its maximum and settle
for a fraction once the coach is rehired. Checkable on the next Power 4 firing with a
published buyout and a rehire within the same cycle. Add to the predictions log. Also sets
