## Post 1 (268/280)

Attach: `figures/cover-fal-1200x630.png`

Penn State's James Franklin buyout was reported at $49.7 million. Penn State paid $9.0 million. The usual telling is a negotiation. The contract says something else: the clause that cut the bill had been in it since 2021, and the clause did most of the work by itself.

## Post 2 (279/280)

Attach: `figures/fig-02b-two-readings-table.png`

Franklin's 2021 contract offset Penn State's payments by his pay at the next job. Read as one offset against the whole term, the clause alone leaves at most $8.0 million. Read year by year, about $10.0 million plus up to $8.0 million for 2031. The $9.0 million deal sits between.

## Post 3 (276/280)

Attach: `figures/fig-02a-year-by-year-table.png`

Virginia Tech pays $6.0 million in 2026, $5.0 million in 2027, $4.0 million in 2028, then $12.75 million and $13.25 million. For the three low years, its pay plus Penn State's offset equals $8.0 million, his guaranteed Penn State pay. Built that way? I can't say from outside.

## Post 4 (263/280)

Penn State kept the clause. Matt Campbell's deal, eight years through 2033 and $70.5 million guaranteed, pays 100% of what's left if he's fired, "subject to mitigation." The mechanism that turned $49.7 million into $9.0 million; its exact wording isn't published.

## Post 5 (263/280)

Attach: `figures/fig-03-against-roster.png`

What the change cost: the $9.0 million settlement plus $2.0 million to Iowa State, $11.0 million. Against The Athletic's $29 million to $32 million roster estimate, the buyout as reported was 1.55 to 1.71 years of roster. The settlement is 28% to 31% of one year.
