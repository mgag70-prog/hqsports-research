# Independent verification of headline figures (Oct 19, 2026)

I checked all 23 items. Fourteen are confirmed, with caveats noted where they apply. The rest need a correction, a qualifier, or the conflict settled. The two that matter most: the $605M Bradbury median is for 2010s venues, not 2020s, and the links Bradbury gives for the referendum statistics don't match what he says they show.

## Do not publish as stated

- Titans: "Apr 25, 2023, 26-11." Use 26-12, early Apr 26.
- Commanders: "$850M DC share." Use about $1.1B (AP).
- Bears: "$5B" as a current cost. Also don't call Indiana's roughly $1B a fixed appropriation.
- Royals: "$274M" state share. Nothing supports it, and the state share isn't final.
- Calgary: "$926.4M (2023) → $1.2B (2026)" as a cost increase. Those are two scopes; also use the City's $515.3M as the 2024 final-agreement figure.
- Lightning: "$250M county." That is only the increase; the total is up to $358.5M.
- Panthers: "$1.3B renovation." It's a sum of contributions that includes past and future capital spending.
- Bradbury: "$605M median public cost for 2020s venues." It's the 2010s median, in 2024 dollars.
- Referendum statistics credited to a named study. Credit Bradbury (The Conversation, 2026) only; his cited links don't support the claims.
- Browns: "court blocked" as the final word. It's a preliminary injunction, and I found nothing after July 2026. Also don't mix $2.4B and $2.6B without naming the scope.
- Bills: "$2.2B" unless you can confirm the SBJ figure. Prefer AP's $2.1B.
- Spurs: don't describe the 56% Prop A vote as an arena vote.

Two DOI lookups (crossref and doi.org) were blocked, so the paper identities in item 23 come from search snippets, not the papers themselves.
