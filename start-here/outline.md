- Ledger & Whistle: the business of sports, by the numbers.
- Every piece starts with a dataset built from public sources and checked line by line; the data and checking scripts are public on GitHub (https://github.com/mgag70-prog/hqsports-research).
- Every piece ends with a prediction on the record, graded in public.
