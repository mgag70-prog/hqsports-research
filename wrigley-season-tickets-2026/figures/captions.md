**fig-04-stat-tiles** (in "The seats and the numbers", after the first paragraph)
Price per seat in Section 105 at three points. The four field box seats cost $4,125 each in 2004, from a total I rounded; $5,849.50 each in 2015, the first year with an exact invoice; and $9,687.75 each in 2021, which works out to about $119.60 a seat for each of the 81 home dates. Nominal dollars. The Section 213 years aren't shown because those totals may include parking and playoff strips. Source: my invoices. Per-seat and per-date figures calculated.

**fig-01-per-seat** (in "The seats and the numbers", after the playoff sentence)
Season-ticket price per seat, 1998 through 2021, in the dollars of each year. The line breaks at 2004 because the seats changed: two in Section 213 through 2003, then four in Section 105 from 2004 on. The two segments aren't the same product, and the Section 213 totals may include parking and playoff strips, so don't read the break as a price comparison. Hollow markers are years whose totals I rounded, some by as much as a couple of thousand dollars; solid markers, from 2015 on, are exact invoices. Per seat, Section 213 went from $4,000 to $5,000 and Section 105 from $4,125 to $9,687.75. Source: my invoices.

**fig-02-real-total** (in "Six years at 6.4% a year above inflation", after the year-by-year sentence)
The Section 105 four-seat season total, 2004 through 2021, restated in 2021 dollars with BLS CPI-U annual averages. From $23,669 in 2004 to $26,750 in 2015 is 1.1% a year; from there to $38,751 in 2021 is 6.4% a year. The rule at 2015 marks the first exact invoice, and the shaded years before it are rounded totals, which is why that period is read from its endpoints only. Source: my invoices; CPI-U from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Real figures and growth rates calculated.

**fig-03-price-vs-cpi** (in "Six years at 6.4% a year above inflation", after the judgment paragraph)
Change in the four-seat total, in navy, beside the change in CPI-U, in gray. The pair at the left is 2004 through 2015 as one compound annual rate, because the totals in those years are rounded and only the endpoints can carry a figure: seats at 3.2% a year against 2.1% for CPI. The six pairs to the right are each exact year over the one before: 15.1%, 8.1%, 8.4%, 4.9%, 8.2% and 8.3%, against CPI that never ran above 4.7% in any of those years. Source: my invoices; CPI-U from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Changes calculated.
